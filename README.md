# Maintenance Performance Analytics

**Business Intelligence | Data Analysis | Maintenance Performance Improvement**

## Project Overview

This project is an independent Business Intelligence case study inspired by my professional experience in industrial maintenance reporting at COFAT, Tunisia.

In my professional role, maintenance activities and performance indicators were monitored through recurring weekly and monthly Excel reports.

To explore opportunities for a more advanced analytical approach, I proposed extending traditional reporting with Business Intelligence tools, particularly Power BI.

The objective was to go beyond periodic reporting and introduce a deeper analysis of maintenance performance over a three-month period, helping identify problematic equipment, understand downtime patterns, prioritize improvement actions, and define measurable performance objectives.

## Professional Context & BI Approach

The improvement approach considered the following analytical objectives:

- **Equipment performance:** identify machines generating the highest downtime and requiring closer monitoring.
- **Downtime analysis:** examine downtime duration, frequency, and distribution.
- **Failure analysis:** identify recurring failure causes and their contribution to equipment downtime.
- **Maintenance strategy:** compare preventive and corrective interventions.
- **Improvement priorities:** identify areas requiring targeted maintenance actions.
- **Performance objectives:** establish measurable indicators to monitor progress and evaluate improvement actions.

This approach aims to complement existing Excel reporting with interactive dashboards and more detailed data analysis.

## Portfolio Case Study & Confidentiality

The GitHub repository contains an independent demonstration of this analytical approach.

Because company maintenance data and internal reports are confidential, the portfolio project uses an entirely synthetic dataset created for demonstration purposes.

The dataset, Python analyses, calculated indicators, and Power BI dashboard are independent portfolio work. They do not reproduce COFAT's internal data, actual equipment performance, or official reporting.

All numerical findings presented in this repository are derived exclusively from the synthetic dataset.
